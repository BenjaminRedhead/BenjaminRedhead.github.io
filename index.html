Research Interests

I am broadly interested in improving time-series foundation models for real-world deployment. Practical time-series data is messy: observations arrive irregularly, channels are multivariate with complex cross-variable dependencies, underlying processes are non-stationary, and useful signal is often buried in noise. My work explores how foundation models can be made robust to these conditions: probabilistic forecasting that accurately quantifies uncertainty, and evaluation methods that capture performance on real-world use cases and important downstream tasks, not average benchmark performance. I am particularly interested in architectures that scale linearly with sequence length, such as SSMs and xLSTM, as efficient alternatives to quadratic-attention transformers for long-horizon time-series modelling.
